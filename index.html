Summary

Notepad Calc is a 100% offline notepad calculator. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect or receive:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details
  • Account or login information (the app has no accounts)
  • Location, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
  • Analytics, crash reports, diagnostics, or usage statistics
  • Payment or financial account information
  • Files, photos, contacts, messages, or clipboard contents sent to us
  • Any other personal or usage data

We do not sell data. We do not use data for advertising, marketing, or profiling. We do not operate a server that receives data from this app.

Information stored on your device

Notes you type, note titles, and your theme preference (light, dark, or system) are saved locally on your device using on-device storage (including a local Room database and local app settings). This data stays on the device unless you yourself copy, share, or export a note through the Android share sheet or clipboard — actions you initiate, sent only to the destination you choose.

You can delete an individual note in the app, or remove all local app data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Android backup and device transfer (operating-system feature)

The app allows Android’s system Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer, so your notes and theme preference can be restored if you set up a new phone or if you have Android backup enabled in your device settings.

This is an operating-system feature controlled by you (for example, Google backup or a device-to-device transfer). It is not developer data collection. UIP does not receive this backup, does not operate a backup server, and cannot read, access, or delete backups stored in your Google account or on your devices. You can turn system backup off in Android settings if you do not want notes included.

Permissions

Notepad Calc requests zero Android permissions. It works fully offline and does not require internet, storage, microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.

Third-party services and SDKs

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, social logins, or other third-party tracking libraries. It uses only standard Android and AndroidX libraries (for example, Jetpack Compose and Room) that run on your device.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to be used by children in a way that would involve collecting their data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new effective date. Because the app collects no personal data, a change will not retroactively collect information that was never gathered.
